# QR Code Options

## Recommended option: unique session URL

Use:

```text
https://luna.wedding/receipts/?id=<session-id>
```

This is the default flow.

Why it is best:

- The QR sends each guest directly to their own receipt strip.
- The full public gallery still appears below.
- The URL is on your wedding domain.
- The public page shows only receipt strips, not raw photos.

## Simpler option: general gallery URL

Use:

```text
https://luna.wedding/receipts/
```

This is simpler, but guests do not land on their own receipt first.

## Current default receipt copy

```text
SEE YOUR PHOTOS
SMILE * SNAP * KEEP
```

These are editable in admin mode.
