Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know for our celebration on Saturday, June 6, 2026.

Where and when is the event? ⌄

Carne Mare at 89 South Street at Pier 17 (behind the Tin Building) on June 6th from 7:00 PM–10:00 PM. Please arrive promptly at 7:00 PM to avoid delaying the event.

How do I get there? ⌄

Carne Mare is on Pier 17, behind the Tin Building, in the Seaport. Cars are not allowed to drive up to the door, so please plan for a short ~1-minute walk from the adjacent sidewalk, down the pier, and past the Tin Building.

By car / rideshare

  • Use 89 South Street, New York, NY 10038 as your destination.
  • Ask to be dropped off near the Seaport / Tin Building / Pier 17 entrance area.
  • From there, walk down the pier to Carne Mare (cars cannot pull up to the restaurant entrance).

By public transit

  • Take the subway to the Fulton Street area (several lines stop nearby).
  • Walk east toward the Seaport / Pier 17 and continue past the Tin Building.
  • Please allow a few extra minutes if it’s your first time navigating the Seaport.
What is parking situation? ⌄

There is no on-site parking, and street parking is limited. We recommend the SpotHero app to book nearby parking garages at low rates.

Who is invited? ⌄

While we unfortunately can't invite all of our friends and loved ones, we did our best to include those that matter the most. Please refer to your invitation for specific invitees to the dinner.

Can I bring my kids, a mutual friend, situationship, or any other uninvited guests? ⌄

Given the smaller capacity, we've had to make difficult choices to be surrounded by those that matter most. Please refrain from any unapproved plus 1's, as they may be turned away from the venue.

Parents — please understand that this is a child-free event.

What is the dress code? ⌄

We request formal, cocktail attire for the reception dinner.

Will there be vows, speeches, music and dancing? ⌄

No — instead, a small, private wedding ceremony will be held prior to this event. We look forward to meeting you all on Saturday, June 6th 2026 at 7:00 PM to celebrate our marriage over delicious food and drink at Carne Mare.

What’s on the menu? ⌄

A special 5-course menu that includes oysters, shrimp, caviar, lobster pasta, and tomahawk steak will be served. You can review Carne Mare's full menu on this page: Carne Mare Dinner Menu. Come hungry!

Please include any food allergies in your RSVP, or use the "Contact the Couple" button from the Menu option on the home page to share any concerns.

Are you registered anywhere? ⌄

Yes we are! Please visit our registry page for more information. Thank you in advance for your generosity!

What time should I arrive? ⌄

Please plan to arrive at 7:00 PM sharp. This helps us start on time and keeps the dinner service flowing smoothly.

What if I have food allergies or dietary restrictions? ⌄

Please include any allergies or dietary restrictions in your RSVP. If you’ve already RSVP’d and need to update us, use the "Contact the Couple" button from the Menu option on the home page.

Can I arrive late? ⌄

We kindly ask everyone to arrive on time. Late arrivals may disrupt the event and dinner service, so please plan ahead for traffic, parking, and the short walk onto Pier 17.

Is there assigned seating? ⌄

Yes — please look for your seating card when you arrive at the venue. Once you've found your place, please scan the QR code at your table for more information on the event and how we can all make it special.

And of course, feel free to visit other tables, grab your reception favors, visit the bar, or take a walk on Pier 17 just outside.

How do I get in touch with you for questions? ⌄

Reach out at any time via email by using the "Contact the Couple" button from the Menu option on the home page.

More details may be added as we get closer. We can’t wait to celebrate with you.
