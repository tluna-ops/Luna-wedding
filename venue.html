How do I get there?

Carne Mare is on Pier 17, behind the Tin Building, in the Seaport. Cars are not allowed to drive directly to the door.

  • Use 89 South Street, New York, NY 10038 as your destination.
  • Ask to be dropped near the Seaport / Tin Building / Pier 17 entrance area.
  • From there, walk down the pier to Carne Mare.
What about public transit?

The closest major subway area is Fulton Street, with additional options around Wall Street. Walk east toward the Seaport / Pier 17 and continue past the Tin Building.

What is the parking situation?

There is no on-site parking, and street parking is limited. We recommend booking a nearby garage ahead of time.
