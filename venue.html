Carne Mare

We’re celebrating at Carne Mare on Pier 17 in Manhattan’s South Street Seaport. Here’s everything you’ll need to get there smoothly on our wedding day, including arrival details, directions, and parking.

Location

Carne Mare
Pier 17, 89 South Street
New York, NY 10038

The restaurant is on Pier 17, behind the Tin Building. Cars cannot pull directly up to the entrance, so please plan for a short walk from the nearby sidewalk or drop-off area.

Getting There

Use 89 South Street, New York, NY 10038 as your destination. Ask to be dropped off near the Seaport / Tin Building / Pier 17 entrance area.

  • Cars are not allowed to drive directly to the Carne Mare entrance.
  • From the drop-off area, walk down the pier to the restaurant.
  • The closest major subway area is Fulton Street, with additional options around Wall Street.
Tip: If you’re taking a taxi or rideshare, tell the driver you’re heading to Pier 17 / the Tin Building in the South Street Seaport.

Parking

There is no on-site parking, and street parking in the Seaport can be limited. To make arrival easier, we recommend reserving a nearby garage ahead of time through SpotHero.
