Carne Mare

We’re celebrating at Carne Mare in Manhattan’s Seaport. Here’s everything you’ll need for arrival, directions, parking, and getting around Pier 17.

Location

Carne Mare – Seaport 89 South Street
New York, NY 10038

Carne Mare is on Pier 17, behind the Tin Building. Cars cannot pull directly up to the restaurant, so please plan for a short walk from the nearby sidewalk or drop-off area.

Getting There

Use 89 South Street, New York, NY 10038 as your destination. Ask to be dropped near the Seaport / Tin Building / Pier 17 entrance area.

  • Cars are not allowed to drive directly to the Carne Mare entrance.
  • From the drop-off area, walk down the pier to Carne Mare.
  • The closest major subway area is Fulton Street, with additional options around Wall Street.

Tip: If you’re taking a taxi or rideshare, tell the driver you’re heading to Pier 17 / the Tin Building at the Seaport.

Parking

There is no on-site parking, and street parking is limited. We recommend booking a nearby garage ahead of time.
