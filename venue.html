Getting There

Subway

The closest major subway area is Fulton Street, with additional options around Wall Street. From there, it’s a short walk toward the Seaport.

Taxi or Rideshare

Traffic near the waterfront can move slowly on weekends. Please give yourself a buffer, especially if you’re arriving close to the start time.

Ferry

Depending on where you’re coming from, the ferry may be a scenic and convenient way to get nearby.

Parking

Street parking in the Seaport is limited. If you’re driving, we recommend using a nearby garage and reserving ahead when possible.

Find Parking Nearby

Garage availability and pricing can change, so guests should confirm directly before arrival.

Accessibility

If you need step-free access, elevator details, seating help, or any other accommodation, please reach out ahead of time so we can help coordinate.

Nearby

The Seaport has waterfront views, cobblestone streets, shops, and places to grab a drink nearby. If you arrive early, it’s a nice area to walk around before heading inside.

It can feel cooler by the water, so consider bringing a light layer if you plan to spend time outside.
